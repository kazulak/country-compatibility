How it works & limitations

Everything this site does, and everything it gets wrong, in one place.

1. What this is

Country Compass is a just-for-fun side project. You say what matters to you, and it ranks countries (and a list of well-known universities) by how well they match. It is not a research tool. The numbers are hand-written guesses, not measurements.

Please don't use this for real decisions. For visas, safety and money, use official government portals, travel advisories and the institutions themselves.

2. How the score works

Each country has a 0–10 number for each of 32 metrics. For every metric you set an importance, which becomes a weight:

  • Off = 0 (ignored)
  • Low = 3
  • Med = 6
  • High = 10

Each metric then gets a match score between 0 and 1, depending on its type:

  • Maximize metrics (higher is better): score = value / 10
  • Preference metrics (you pick a target): score = 1 − |target − value| / 10

The total is the weighted average of those scores, times 100, rounded:

total = 100 × sum(score × weight) / sum(weight)

Metrics that are Off, or that have no data for a country, are skipped rather than guessed. If every metric is skipped the total is 100.

Worked example. You set Warm Weather (preference) to a target of 8 with High importance, and Safety (maximize) with Med importance. A country has warm weather 6 and safety 9.

  • Warm weather: 1 − |8 − 6| / 10 = 0.80, weight 10
  • Safety: 9 / 10 = 0.90, weight 6
  • Total: (0.80 × 10 + 0.90 × 6) / (10 + 6) = 13.4 / 16 = 0.8375, so 84
The model is simple and has not been validated against anything. It is linear, has no interactions between metrics, uses arbitrary weights (3/6/10), and "preference" metrics treat being over your target exactly the same as being under it (a country 2 points too hot scores the same as one 2 points too cold).

5. Known problems and likely errors

  • Templates fit some countries badly. China, South Korea, Uzbekistan and Azerbaijan use the "Temperate Europe" template. Kenya, Nigeria and Ethiopia use "Tropical (Latin America)". The "Gulf / Desert" template claims zero income tax and high safety for Egypt, Algeria, Libya and others. Ukraine inherits "high public safety".
  • Template countries ignore current events. They do not reflect conflicts, travel advisories or sanctions.
  • Countries sharing a template tie exactly, because their scores are identical.
  • Visa rules change often. The visa text on curated countries may be out of date, and template visa text is generic.
  • Scores are integers from 0 to 10 written by one person. There is no second opinion and no inter-rater check.
  • Several universities share an approximate rank, and ranks beyond 100 are only bands.
  • University scores describe the country, not the university.
  • The quiz presets are arbitrary. They are convenient starting points, not findings.
  • Labels like "Happiness" or "Safety" do not match any official index.
